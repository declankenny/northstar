# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Northstar to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps build a welcoming community:

- Being kind and patient with other people, especially newcomers.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Posting real financial data, whether yours or anyone else's, in an issue, a pull request or a screenshot.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behaviour they find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. That can include removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, issues and other contributions, and explaining why.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private report form: <https://github.com/declankenny/northstar/security/advisories/new>. Say that it is a conduct report, not a security one, and include links or screenshots of what happened. The form is built for security reports, but it reaches only the maintainer, which is what is needed here. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what follows, in proportion to what happened, from a private conversation and a warning, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>. It is shortened, and the enforcement ladder is simplified for a project with one maintainer.
